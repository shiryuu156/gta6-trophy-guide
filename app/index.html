Roadmap to the Platinum

About this guide

This guide covers the official PlayStation 5 trophy list for Grand Theft Auto VI: 55 trophies — 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver and 46 bronze — of which 28 are hidden on PlayStation until earned. Every trophy's name, description and grade are shown as PlayStation publishes them. Every guide is written in our own words for this entry, and each one names the published guides its facts come from, so you can check any claim. Where the guides disagree or a method is still unconfirmed, the guide says so instead of guessing.

How to use the tracker

  • Tick a trophy's box when you earn it. Progress is saved in your browser (localStorage) and survives closing the page.
  • Export progress downloads a JSON file; Import progress reads one back. The share link encodes your progress in the page address, so you can send it to another device.
  • Spoiler masks hide the names and descriptions of the 28 hidden story trophies until you earn them or reveal them one by one. Turn the masks off to see the whole list.
  • Filters narrow the list by grade, kind, difficulty, status and missable flag; search matches names, descriptions and guide text.
  • The whole guide works with the network off: it is plain files, and nothing is fetched at run time except its own data files.
