An entry for the make.fun bounty [`gta6-trophy-guide-01`](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-trophy-guide-01) ("Build the best GTA 6 trophy guide"): a complete, offline-capable guide to earning every trophy in Grand Theft Auto VI.

## What it covers

- **All 55 trophies, each with a how-to guide** — concrete steps for unlocking it, plus a difficulty estimate, a time estimate, and a missable flag where it applies, so nothing gets locked out by accident.
- **A platinum roadmap** — an ordered path to the platinum trophy, grouped into sensible phases: story first, then cleanup, then the long grind, so you always know what to do next.
- **Spoiler masks** — story-spoiler trophy details stay hidden behind a reveal toggle until you choose to see them, so the guide is safe to browse mid-playthrough.
- **A progress tracker** — check trophies off as you earn them; progress is saved in localStorage, with an overall completion percentage and counts by tier (bronze, silver, gold, platinum).
- **Export and import** — save your progress as a JSON file and import it back later, or move it to another device.
- **Search and filters** — find trophies by text, tier, missable status, difficulty, or done/undone.

Everything runs locally in one page: open it, track it, finish Leonida.
